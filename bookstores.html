bookstores

  • City Lights - 9.5/10 excellent philosophy and poetry (whole room!) collection, three stories.
  • Barnes and Noble Santa Clara - 8.5/10 Happy childhood hours.
  • Cuppa Tahoe - 8/10 Small selection but immaculate vibes, good coffee.
  • Bell’s Books - 8/10 very cozy, sort of maze-like, has old books and includes a Richard Rorty’s personal collection.
  • Barnes and Noble Los Gatos - 7.5/10 Book covers are all modern and aesthetic, not much character.
  • Green Apple Books on the Park - 7/10 weaker poetry, seems more commercial.
  • Barnes and Noble Almaden (closed) - 7/10 Excursions in early high school.
  • Books, Inc. - 5/10 Small, poetry is very weak.
